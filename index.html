About

Nice to meet you

Hello! I am a bilingual Portuguese and English UGC creator, millennial working mom and finance professional based in Canada.

As a seasoned UGC creator, I have been trusted to create content for brands like L'Oréal, Niche Beauty Lab and TheraBreath. My 2+ years of content creation span beauty, wellness, lifestyle and home decor, and are not limited to those niches.

With 15+ years of professional experience in finance and the corporate world, I bring you a deep understanding of brand positioning and consumer psychology: the foundation behind high converting content.

Before creating, I identify the emotion the content needs to trigger, relief, desire, trust, confidence or curiosity, and connect it to the consumer's real life routine.
